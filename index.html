// The Philosophy

What is "the in-between moment"?

It's the five or ten minutes between the bell ringing and your next class starting. It's not free time to fill up your whole afternoon — it's a short, specific window that exists whether you use it or not.

DontSkipClass is built for exactly that window. Quick to load, quick to play, and easy to close the second the next bell rings. The goal was never to give you a reason to stay out longer — it's to make sure the time you already have doesn't go to waste.

Used right, a few minutes of a game between classes is a nice reset. Used wrong, "just five more minutes" turns into a habit of skipping the class itself — and that's the one thing this site was never meant to encourage.

// Why You Shouldn't Skip Class

A few honest reminders before you head out

  1. Missing Key Explanations: Teachers often drop crucial hints about upcoming tests or break down complex problems in ways that textbooks just can't match.
  2. Snowballing Catch-Up Work: Skipping one single class turns into spending hours later trying to track down notes and teach yourself from scratch.
  3. Attendance & Participation Points: A lot of classes bake attendance directly into your grade. Skipping means throwing away free percentage points.
  4. Breaking the Routine: Once you break the habit of showing up, it gets way easier to justify skipping the next time, leading straight into a slump.
  5. Losing Access to the Instructor: Office hours and quick after-class questions are way easier to get when you're a familiar face who actually shows up.
  6. Group Work Fallout: Missed classes often mean missed group assignments, leaving teammates to cover for you and hurting the trust you've built.
