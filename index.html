// Why You Shouldn't Skip Class
A few reminders before you head out
📚

Missing Key Explanations

Teachers often drop crucial hints about upcoming tests or break down complex problems in ways that textbooks just can't match.

🔁

Snowballing Catch-Up Work

Skipping one single class turns into spending hours later trying to track down notes and teach yourself from scratch.

🎯

Attendance & Participation Points

A lot of classes bake attendance directly into your grade. Skipping means throwing away free percentage points.

🔄

Breaking the Routine

Once you break the habit of showing up, it gets way easier to justify skipping the next time, leading straight into a slump.

🧑‍🏫

Losing Access to the Instructor

Office hours and quick after-class questions are way easier to get when you're a familiar face who actually shows up. Skip enough and that access quietly disappears.

🤝

Group Work Fallout

Missed classes often mean missed group assignments or project updates, leaving teammates to cover for you and hurting the trust you've built with them.

About DontSkipClass

DontSkipClass is a free, no-signup break-time site built for the in-between moments of the school day — a quick distraction that doesn't ask for an account, a subscription, or your personal info. Everything here runs entirely in your browser, and the small ads you see are what keep it free to use.

The reminders above aren't meant to lecture you — they're just honest nudges. A few minutes of fun between classes is great; just make sure it doesn't turn into skipping the class itself.
