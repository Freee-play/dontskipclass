About DontSkipClass

A free, no-signup site for the few minutes between one class and the next.

What it is

DontSkipClass is a small break-time games site. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, and nothing to download. You open it, hit continue, and you're in.

The in-between moment

The site is built around one idea: the short window between the bell and your next class. Long enough to reset your head, short enough that you're back in your seat on time.

That's also why the loading screen has a countdown and a few reminders in it. A quick game is a good break. An open-ended one is how people end up skipping the class itself, and this site was never meant to encourage that.

Who it's for

It's made by one person for a group of friends. It's free, it isn't trying to sell you anything, and it doesn't ask for your personal information.
